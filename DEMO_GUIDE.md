# Demo Guide

Start at `/demo`. Choose a guided story, then use Next and Previous to progress through the customer, associate and manager narrative.

## Stories

1. Quiet Store — a recommended overshirt receives immediate staff delivery.
2. 32 Rooms, One Colleague — companion collection and batching relieve pressure.
3. Stock Discrepancy — an unavailable item becomes an alternative and a missed-demand signal.
4. Batch Pick — three rooms share one stockroom route.

Use Reset before beginning a new story. Presentation mode removes surrounding chrome. Presenter notes describe the operational problem, retailer question and metric at each step.
