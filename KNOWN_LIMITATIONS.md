# Known Limitations

- This is a browser prototype with no production backend, authentication, payments or retailer integration.
- Data resets on refresh except for the selected scenario stored locally in the browser.
- Availability, timings, routes and analytics are deterministic demonstrations rather than live operational facts.
- Lead-form entries remain in the local session and are not submitted anywhere.
- The ROI-style planning figure is a caveated missed-demand model, not a forecast, guarantee, savings claim or conversion claim.
- RFID is optional and is not required by the prototype.
